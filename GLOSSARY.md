# Buddha TikTok Live Sanctuary

An interactive 3D Buddhist prayer and meditation live streaming system on TikTok Live, connecting viewers to a virtual temple of Bodhisattva Guan Yin with authentic rituals and merit rewards.

## Language

**Devotee**:
A live viewer assigned to a meditation cushion in the sanctuary.
_Avoid_: Spectator, customer, player, NPC

**Sanctuary Seat**:
One of the 16 designated meditation cushions flanking the central red carpet aisle within the safe viewing frustum.
_Avoid_: Slot, tile, bench, chair

**Room Join Allocation**:
The automatic assignment of an available sanctuary seat to a newly arrived live viewer, seating them in quiet meditation.
_Avoid_: Spawn, spawn-on-follow, connect hook

**Follow Blessing**:
The sacred ritual performed when a seated devotee follows the host, triggering a Devotional Bow and Golden Blessing Aura at their seat.
_Avoid_: Follow spawn, follow join

**Devotional Rose Tribute**:
The 1-coin rose offering ceremony where the devotee walks down the central red carpet aisle to perform the 3-step Benjangkhapradit kowtow before Guan Yin.
_Avoid_: Incense gift, candle gift

**Lotus Bloom Tribute**:
The 5-coin floral offering that manifests a radiant multi-layer sacred lotus at the foot of the altar without obscuring the deity.
_Avoid_: Flower spawn, altar bloom

**Auspicious Petal Shower**:
The 10+ coin major merit event releasing a gentle, fluttering cascade of 48 multi-colored lotus and jasmine petals across the sanctuary.
_Avoid_: Petal rain storm, confetti

**Top Donor Podium**:
The minimalist glassmorphism HUD widget positioned in the Top-Right corner displaying the top merit donors.
_Avoid_: Bottom drawer, bottom leaderboard

**Tribute Guide**:
The rotating ticker widget positioned in the Top-Left corner displaying the merit tribute rewards.
_Avoid_: Recommendation banner, floating toast
